### **THE OPPORTUNITY**

**SciMSPT Quantum** is a unified quantum chemistry simulation platform providing single-interface access to **IBM Qiskit, Google Cirq, Microsoft Azure Quantum, Rigetti, IonQ, and Xanadu**. We're democratizing quantum computing for researchers, startups, and enterprises who need molecular simulation capabilities but cannot navigate the fragmented quantum ecosystem alone.

---

### **THE PROBLEM**

Today's quantum computing landscape is **fragmented across 6+ major vendors**, each with:
- Proprietary SDKs (Qiskit, Cirq, Q#, Quil, PennyLane)
- Different pricing models and access restrictions
- Steep learning curves requiring quantum programming expertise
- No chemistry-specific optimization or templates

**Result:** Researchers spend months learning platforms instead of doing science. Startups delay quantum initiatives due to complexity. Enterprises struggle to evaluate which technology to bet on.
