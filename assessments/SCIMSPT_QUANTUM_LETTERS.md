## Usage Guidelines

### Customization Best Practices

1. **Research the recipient thoroughly** before sending — customize every bracketed section with specific details
2. **Keep tone professional but warm** — quantum computing is exciting; convey enthusiasm without hype
3. **Be honest about limitations** — credibility matters more than closing every deal
4. **Include clear calls to action** — specify exactly what next step you want
5. **Follow up persistently but respectfully** — 3-7 touches typically needed before response

### Timing Considerations

- **Investors:** Avoid end-of-quarter, holidays, or known busy periods
- **Academics:** Beginning of semester (Aug/Sep, Jan) or after major conferences
- **Startups:** Anytime; they move fast but respect their time constraints
- **Enterprises:** Budget planning cycles (Q3/Q4 for next year); avoid fiscal year-end closings

### Legal/Compliance Notes

- Include appropriate disclaimers about forward-looking statements
- Ensure compliance with anti-spam regulations (CAN-SPAM, GDPR)
- For enterprise proposals, involve legal counsel before sending
- Protect confidential information; use NDAs appropriately
