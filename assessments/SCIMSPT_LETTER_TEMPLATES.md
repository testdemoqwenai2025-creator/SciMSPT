## Usage Guidelines

### Customization Instructions

1. **Replace all bracketed text** `[LIKE THIS]` with your specific information
2. **Adjust tone** based on recipient and relationship stage
3. **Keep letters concise** (maximum 1-2 pages unless specified)
4. **Proofread carefully** before sending
5. **Save versions** for tracking purposes
6. **Convert to PDF** when formal presentation is required

### When to Use PDF vs. Email Format

| **Situation** | **Recommended Format** |
|--------------|----------------------|
| Initial cold outreach | Email (shorter, less formal) |
| Formal application/proposal | PDF attachment + email cover |
| Follow-up to meeting | Email (reference previous discussion) |
| Contract/legal matters | PDF (formal record) |
| Investor updates | Email (regular cadence) |
| Press/media inquiries | PDF (professional presentation) |
